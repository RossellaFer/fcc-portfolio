About

Hi, I’m Rossella.

I’m a software engineer based in Tokyo and a Frontend Tech Lead at Flagship LLC. I specialize in Shopify development and building maintainable storefronts that connect customer needs, business goals, and technical systems.

Over seven years in web development, I’ve led projects, co-founded the sustainable e-commerce agency Nama Studio, and worked across engineering, UX, and performance. My earlier career in marketing still shapes how I translate requirements into practical products.

I also contribute to Women in Software Engineering Japan and write about technology, leadership, and life between Europe and Japan.
